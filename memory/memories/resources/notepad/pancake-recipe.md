### Pancake Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon white sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt (or more to taste)
- 1 ¼ cups milk
- 3 tablespoons butter (melted)
- 1 large egg

**Serving Suggestion:**
Best served with orange juice for a refreshing start to your morning.
